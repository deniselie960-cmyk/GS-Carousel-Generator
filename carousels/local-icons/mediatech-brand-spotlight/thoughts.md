# Mediatech Brand Spotlight — Development Notes

## Objective

Create a six-slide Local Icons Brand Spotlight carousel for Mediatech at `1080 × 1350px`, exported as sharp PNG files at `2160 × 2700px`.

## Non-negotiable requirements

- Preserve the supplied copy exactly, including spelling, punctuation, slash usage, and English phrasing.
- Do not add product names, specifications, prices, claims, headlines, URLs, or promotional conditions.
- Preserve the supplied Mediatech logo and product photography without recoloring or distortion.
- Keep the solid black backgrounds in the supplied product images and incorporate them as deliberate dark product cards.
- Use the approved Local Icons lockups without modification.
- Maintain the Local Icons white-led visual system, safe margins, Gotham typography, soft rounded forms, and bright campaign accents.
- Keep primary copy, logos, and product cards visibly separated.

## Visual direction

The carousel uses a clean technology-editorial direction based on the existing BARDI Brand Spotlight, adapted to the Mediatech teal logo and black product photography. White and warm-cream fields remain dominant. Teal leads the accent system; purple, blue, and yellow provide carousel rhythm.

The black photography is not treated as a limitation or artificially removed. It becomes a consistent premium-tech surface inside rounded cards with restrained shadows and colored supporting panels.
