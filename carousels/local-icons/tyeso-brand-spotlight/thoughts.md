# TYESO Brand Spotlight — Development Notes

## Objective

Create a five-slide Local Icons Brand Spotlight carousel for TYESO at `1080 × 1350px`, exported as sharp PNG files at `2160 × 2700px`.

## Non-negotiable requirements

- Preserve the supplied visible copy exactly, including spelling, capitalization, punctuation, percentages, and pricing shorthand.
- Treat wording embedded in source photographs as image content, not additional requested copy. Crop away unrequested claims where practical.
- Do not add dates, venue details, URLs, CTAs, product names, capacities, or promotional conditions.
- Preserve the supplied TYESO logo and product photography without recoloring or distortion.
- Use the approved Local Icons horizontal lockup on every slide.
- Maintain the Local Icons white/cream-led system, safe margins, Gotham typography, soft rounded forms, and campaign accents.
- Keep primary copy, logos, and photographs visibly separated.

## Approved visual direction

The direction is bright modern drinkware editorial: clean white and cool cream fields, TYESO blue as a restrained brand anchor, and Local Icons teal, purple, blue, and yellow as supporting accents. Product silhouettes and lifestyle photography should lead without allowing baked-in source-image claims to become carousel messaging.

- Local Icons horizontal lockup at the top.

### Slide 3 — Product showcase

- No visible title or added copy.

### Slide 4 — Product showcase
