# Unitech Brand Spotlight — Development Notes

## Objective

Create a four-slide Local Icons Brand Spotlight carousel for Unitech at `1080 × 1350px`, exported as sharp PNG files at `2160 × 2700px`.

## Non-negotiable requirements

- Preserve all supplied copy exactly, including spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and English phrasing.
- Do not add product names, specifications, prices, claims, headlines, URLs, promotional conditions, or CTA copy.
- Preserve the supplied Unitech logo and product images without recoloring or distortion.
- Keep the product images' original white fields and incorporate them into the white-led Local Icons visual system.
- Use the approved Local Icons horizontal lockup without modification.
- Keep primary copy, logos, and product imagery visibly separated.

## Visual direction

The carousel uses a bright technology-editorial direction. Unitech blue leads the brand moments while Local Icons teal, purple, yellow, and blue create rhythm at the edges. Product imagery is arranged in rounded, softly shadowed cards that merge naturally with the source images' white backgrounds.
