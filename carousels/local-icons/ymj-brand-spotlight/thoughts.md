# YMJ Brand Spotlight — Development Notes

## Objective

Create a five-slide Local Icons Brand Spotlight carousel for YMJ at `1080 × 1350px`, exported as sharp PNG files at `2160 × 2700px`.

## Non-negotiable requirements

- Preserve the supplied visible copy exactly, including spelling, capitalization, punctuation, abbreviations, and English phrasing.
- Omit the stray whitespace entity; it is not visible copy.
- Do not add product names, prices, dates, URLs, promotional conditions, or claims.
- Preserve the supplied YMJ logo and product photography without recoloring or distortion.
- Use the approved Local Icons horizontal lockup on every slide.
- Maintain the Local Icons white/cream-led system, safe margins, Gotham typography, soft rounded forms, and campaign accents.
- Keep primary copy, logos, and photographs visibly separated.

## Visual direction

The direction is contemporary Indonesian fashion editorial. Warm cream and white backgrounds echo the supplied studio photography. Deep oxblood, terracotta, pale blue, teal, purple, and yellow accents are used selectively around the Local Icons system without competing with the garments.

Photography leads Slides 3 and 4. The cover balances a large red look with a restrained YMJ logo window. Slide 2 pairs the supplied paragraph with a full-length black floral look. Slide 5 treats the goodie-bag message as the primary offer and does not invent or depict a specific bag because no goodie-bag image was supplied.
